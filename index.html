Your website design journey

I keep you involved at every stage, so you're happy with the finished result before you pay.

  1. Tell me about your business

    Share your business details, services, contact information, logo, photographs, preferred colours and examples of websites you like. Unsure about the design or wording? I'll help you work out what suits your business.

  2. I design your website

    I create a site tailored to your business: professional, easy to navigate, great on phones and computers, with clear information about your services and simple ways for customers to contact you.

  3. See your website before you commit

    I send you a link so you can explore the design online, check the wording and see how it looks on your phone or computer.

    No upfront payment. No obligation while you review.

  4. Request any changes

    Colours, wording, sections, photographs or other agreed adjustments: tell me and I'll make them, then send you an updated link.

  5. Keep refining until you're happy

    We repeat the review and changes until you feel confident about how your website represents your business.

  6. Approve the finished website and pay

    Once you've approved the final design and confirmed you're completely happy, you pay the agreed £100. You know the price before the work begins.

  7. Receive your completed website

    You get the website files and straightforward instructions for publishing online, using a free hosting option so there's no monthly hosting fee. I can guide you through it, or do the publishing for you.

  8. Learn how to manage your website

    I show you how to make simple changes yourself, like updating text, replacing photographs or changing contact details, so you don't pay me every time.

  9. Optional ongoing support

    Prefer to leave the technical side to me? An optional monthly maintenance service is available for a small additional fee. See below.
